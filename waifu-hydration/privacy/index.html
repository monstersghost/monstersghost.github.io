Waifu Hydration

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Waifu Hydration ("the app") is made by monstersghost, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Short version: your water log stays between you and your waifu.

What the app stores

  • How many glasses of water you logged each day
  • Your settings: daily goal, reminder times or interval, quiet hours, precise timing and appearance (System, Light or Dark)

All of this is stored on your phone. The app has no account, no servers of its own and no internet access.

Backups

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, so your history can come back on a new phone. That backup is kept by Google in your account. We can't see or access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your phone. A copy may stay in your phone's backup in your Google account until Android replaces or removes it. You can manage backups in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show hydration reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional): lets reminders arrive exactly on time.
  • Run at startup: restores your reminders after the phone restarts.

Data collection and sharing

The app doesn't collect, send, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data, for example by adding ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
