Waifu Hydration

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Waifu Hydration ("the app") is made by monstersghost, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Short version: your water log lives on your phone, not with us. The app shows a small banner ad from Google, and Google's ad software collects some data about your device to do that; details below.

What the app stores

  • How many glasses of water you logged each day
  • Your settings: daily goal, reminder times or interval, quiet hours, precise timing and appearance (System, Light or Dark)
  • Reminder scheduling details, including your time zone, so reminders arrive at the right local time

All of this is stored on your phone. The app has no account and no servers of its own, and tracking and reminders work offline.

Ads

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app automatically collects and shares:

  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • how you use the app, such as app launches and taps on ads
  • diagnostics, such as app performance
  • device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data goes to Google, encrypted in transit, and is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We don't receive it; AdMob only shows us overall ad statistics. Your water log and settings are never part of it.

If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings > About > Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Backups

Android backup is on by default, and the app doesn't exclude its data from it. Depending on your phone and settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account's backup or move it to a new phone during setup. We never receive these backups. You can manage backup in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes its data from your phone. A copy may remain in your backup or on another phone, and reinstalling may restore it. You can manage backups in your phone's settings. Data collected by Google's ad software is handled by Google; see the link above.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show hydration reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional): lets reminders arrive exactly on time.
  • Vibration: lets reminders vibrate.
  • Run at startup: restores your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and, where required, the consent message.
  • Advertising ID: lets Google's ad software read your resettable advertising ID.
  • Keep awake and background tasks: used by Google's ad software for short background work.

Data collection and sharing

The app doesn't send your water log or settings to us or to anyone else. It has no analytics or crash-reporting code of its own. Google's ad software collects and shares the data described under Ads.

Purchases

This version has no purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
